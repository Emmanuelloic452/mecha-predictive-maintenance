
# MECHA — Predictive Maintenance for CNC Machines

## 1. Project Overview
MECHA is a fictional industrial company specializing in the manufacturing of precision mechanical parts for clients in the aerospace and automotive sectors.

Computer Numerical Control (CNC) machines may experience failures affecting components such as spindles, bearings, or motors, potentially disrupting production.

This project aims to develop a machine learning system that estimates the risk of CNC machine failure within the next 48 hours, helping maintenance teams plan inspections and preventive interventions.

## 2. Business Problem
Unexpected CNC machine failures can cause production slowdowns, unplanned downtime, and additional maintenance and repair costs.

The primary business objectives are to reduce unplanned downtime, improve maintenance planning, and control operational costs.

The proposed system aims to provide risk alerts that help maintenance managers assess machine conditions and decide whether preventive inspections or maintenance interventions are necessary.
